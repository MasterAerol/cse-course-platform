# Number Problems Teaching System v1

Authoritative content: `scripts/lib/number-problems-teaching-system-content.mjs`  
Safe publisher: `scripts/create-and-publish-number-problems-teaching-system.mjs`  
Registry topic: `number-problems`

The audited curriculum contains 12 ordered activities: Translating Number Statements; Consecutive Integers; Consecutive Odd and Even Integers; Sum and Difference of Numbers; Product and Quotient Relationships; Two-Digit Number Problems; Reversed-Digit Problems; Number and Remainder Problems; Fractional Parts of Numbers; Mixed Number Relationship Problems; Mixed Number Problems Practice; and Number Problems Topic Quiz.

Nine generated-practice mappings, the fixed eight-question mixed practice, the fixed 15-question quiz, lesson routes, curriculum order, and Smart Recovery mappings remain owned by the existing topic source and are not mutated by the v1 content publisher. The publisher only reconciles lesson blocks through a topic-restricted endpoint. Any excess or illustrated-guided block is reported with its ID, position, type, identifier, reason, and `requires-human-review` classification. No deletion is automatically approved.
